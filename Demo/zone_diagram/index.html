Zone diagram sweep

Each zone Ri is the set of points at least as close to its site pi as to every other zone. The zones are grown from their sites and each boundary ray is fixed in increasing order of its distance to the site, like Dijkstra's algorithm. No pixel grid and no global iteration. The α slider moves continuously from the Voronoi diagram to the zone diagram.

Each zone competes with the other zones shrunk towards their sites, Sj = pj + α (Rj − pj). At α = 0 the competitors are the sites alone and the zones are the Voronoi cells.
